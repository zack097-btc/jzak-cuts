The preview retraces as you drag. Despeckle throws away dust and scanner grain; Denoise softens JPEG mush before the edges are found; Drop the background color knocks out the paper or backdrop the art was shot on.
SVG, DXF, PDF and EPS come in as real vectors. Silhouette .studio3 imports its actual cut lines — no tracing. Images are auto-traced; colored art comes in as one layer per vinyl color, in registration. A PDF or EPS with live text has no outlines to cut — convert the type to outlines first and it will import.

Cutter Connection

Leave this on Automatic. It now asks for XON/XOFF first, which is the only handshaking a 3-wire USB lead can actually carry, and only falls back to RTS/CTS and then none if that is refused. If a cut still stops part way through a sheet of two or more, set XON/XOFF outright and drop Send speed to Slow.

The send is already paced to the speed of the wire, so the cutter is never handed more than it can read. This adds extra delay on top for a machine with a very small buffer. It costs nothing but time.
